Dua for Breaking Fast (Iftar)
ذَهَبَ الظَّمَأُ وَابْتَلَّتِ الْعُرُوقُ وَثَبَتَ الْأَجْرُ إِنْ شَاءَ اللَّهُ
Dhahaba adh-dhama'u, wabtallat al-'urooqu, wa thabatal-ajru in sha Allah
"The thirst is gone, the veins are moistened, and the reward is confirmed, if Allah wills."

Wa bi sawmi ghadin nawaytu min shahri Ramadan

"O Allah, You are the Most Forgiving, and You love to forgive, so forgive me."

💡 Daily Ramadan Tips
💧
Stay Hydrated
Drink plenty of water between Iftar and Suhoor. Aim for 8 glasses. Avoid excessive caffeine — it dehydrates you during the fast.
🥗
Balanced Iftar
Break your fast with dates and water, then eat a balanced meal. Avoid overeating — the Prophet (PBUH) said: "The son of Adam fills no vessel worse than his stomach."
😴
Sleep Schedule
Take a short nap (Qailulah) in the afternoon if possible. The Prophet (PBUH) recommended this. It helps maintain energy for Taraweeh prayers.
📖
Read Quran Daily
Set a realistic daily Quran goal. Even 1 page per prayer = 5 pages/day = 1 Juz in 6 days. The Quran was revealed in Ramadan — honor it.
🤲
Increase Duas & Dhikr
The Prophet (PBUH) said the dua of a fasting person is not rejected. Make abundant dua especially before Iftar. Repeat: "Allahumma innaka 'afuwwun tuhibbul-'afwa fa'fu 'anni"
💝
Give Sadaqah Daily
Even a smile is charity. The Prophet (PBUH) was the most generous in Ramadan. Give what you can — food, money, time, or kind words.

🕌 I'tikaf (Spiritual Retreat)

What is I'tikaf?

I'tikaf is the practice of secluding oneself in the mosque for worship, especially during the last 10 days of Ramadan. The Prophet (PBUH) practiced this every Ramadan.

Rules: Stay in the mosque, focus on Quran and prayer, minimize worldly talk, no leaving except for necessity (bathroom, wudu, food if unavailable in mosque).

Best time: Last 10 nights of Ramadan — to seek Laylat al-Qadr.

For women: Can be done at home in a designated prayer space if mosque access isn't available.

🤲 Additional Ramadan Duas
Dua After Eating at Iftar
اللَّهُمَّ صُمْتُ لَكَ وَبِكَ آمَنْتُ وَعَلَى رِزْقِكَ أَفْطَرْتُ
Allahumma sumtu laka wa bika aamantu wa 'ala rizqika aftartu
"O Allah, I fasted for You and with Your belief I broke my fast, and on Your provision I broke my fast."
Dua for Forgiveness in Ramadan
رَبَّنَا ظَلَمْنَا أَنْفُسَنَا وَإِنْ لَمْ تَغْفِرْ لَنَا لَنَكُونَنَّ مِنَ الْخَاسِرِينَ
Rabbana zalamna anfusana wa in lam taghfir lana lanakunanna minal-khasirin
"Our Lord, we have wronged ourselves, and if You do not forgive us and have mercy upon us, we will surely be among the losers." — Surah Al-A'raf 7:23
Dua for Acceptance of Worship
رَبَّنَا تَقَبَّلْ مِنَّا إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ السَّمِيعُ الْعَلِيمُ
Rabbana taqabbal minna innaka antas-Sami'ul-'Alim
"Our Lord, accept it from us. Indeed You are the Hearing, the Knowing." — Based on Surah Al-Baqarah 2:127
Dua for Guidance & Steadfastness
رَبَّنَا لَا تُزِغْ قُلُوبَنَا بَعْدَ إِذْ هَدَيْتَنَا وَهَبْ لَنَا مِنْ لَدُنْكَ رَحْمَةً
Rabbana la tuzigh quloobana ba'da idh hadaytana wa hab lana min ladunka rahmah
"Our Lord, do not let our hearts deviate after You have guided us, and grant us from Yourself mercy." — Surah Al Imran 3:8
