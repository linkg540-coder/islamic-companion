Privacy Policy

Islamic Companion · last updated 16 November 2026

Islamic Companion is free to use. Prayer times, the Quran, qibla and the rest of the core tools stay free and stay ad-free. To cover the cost of hosting and AI answers, a small number of ads appear on some other screens, and this version of the policy adds the cookie and advertising disclosures that go with them.

Advertising has not been switched on everywhere yet. The advertising and cookie sections below take effect as it rolls out, and they already describe exactly how it will work.

What stays on your device

Settings, bookmarks, tasbih counts, journal entries, check-ins and cached Quran text are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. They are not uploaded anywhere unless you create a cloud-sync account. No advertiser can read them: ads are rendered in a separate frame and never receive your app data.

Location

Your location is used to calculate prayer times and the qibla direction. If you grant location access, coordinates are sent to the Aladhan prayer-times API and to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service to name your town. If you decline, the app uses a rough IP-based estimate from ipinfo.io or ipwho.is. Coordinates are included in prayer-alert settings only when you enable notifications. Your precise coordinates are never sent to an advertising network.

Optional cloud sync

If you create an account, the app stores your email address, a salted PBKDF2 hash of your password (never the password itself), a session token, and a backup copy of your app data. The backup is used only to restore your data on your own devices. Login events (time, email, device type) are recorded for account security.

Notifications

If you enable prayer or journal alerts, your browser's push subscription, timezone, approximate coordinates and alert preferences are stored so the reminder can be delivered. You can turn alerts off at any time with the bell icon, or in your device settings.

AI Sheikh queries

When you ask the AI Sheikh a question, the text of that question is sent to Groq, which runs the GPT-OSS-120B model that writes the answer. Questions are not tied to your account or used to build a profile of you, and they are not used with your journal, prayer logs or location. Please don't type personal, medical or financial details into it.

Analytics

The app uses GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service, to count page visits so we know whether people find the app useful. It sets no cookies, collects no personal data, builds no profile of you, and does not track you across other websites. It records only an anonymous page view: the path you visited, the referring site, the country your request came from, and your browser and screen size. No IP addresses are stored and no advertising networks are involved.

Advertising and cookies

Ads are served through Google AdSense and may also be served through other Google-certified advertising partners. Where ads are shown, these third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or to other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

Which screens carry ads: advertisements appear throughout the app, one at the bottom of each screen below the content and its controls, each labelled Advertisement. Our own rule, which is stricter than the policy requires, is that no ad ever appears inside the in-app Quran reader, on the standalone Quran reading pages at islamiccompanion.net/quran/, on the Qibla compass or on the prayer-times screen, and no ad interrupts a notification.

Your advertising choices

  • Turn off personalised advertising from Google at My Ad Center.
  • Opt out of participating companies' personalised ads at aboutads.info or optout.networkadvertising.org.
  • Block or clear cookies in your browser settings. Ads will still appear, but they will be less relevant.
  • Where a browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information.

Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent message appears before any personalised advertising loads, and ads remain non-personalised unless you actively agree. You can change your choice at any time using the advertising settings on this page, and refusing it does not limit any feature of the app. Our legal basis for personalised advertising is your consent; for the rest of the app it is the performance of the service you asked for, or our legitimate interest in keeping it running.

Visitors in California

We do not sell your personal information for money. Advertising partners may use cookies that count as "sharing" under the CPRA, which is why the opt-out choices above are available to everyone, not only California residents. You can also email us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of the information we hold about you. We respond within 45 days.

Your rights

  • Access and portability — request a copy of what a cloud-sync account holds.
  • Correction — fix an inaccurate email address or profile detail.
  • Deletion — remove a sync account and its backup, or clear the local data on a device.
  • Objection — refuse personalised advertising without losing access to anything.

To exercise any of these, email linkg540@gmail.com from the address you registered with.

Third-party services

  • Aladhan — prayer times and qibla calculation
  • AlQuran.cloud — Quran text, translations and recitation audio
  • cdn.islamic.network / everyayah.com — recitation audio
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim, Overpass) — place search and nearby mosques
  • jsDelivr — tafsir text
  • Base44 — account sync, backups and notification delivery
  • Groq (GPT-OSS-120B) — generates AI Sheikh answers from the text of your question
  • Google AdSense and advertising partners — serve and measure the ads described above
  • GoatCounter — cookieless visit counting

Each of these services receives only what is needed for its request. Aside from the advertising cookies described above, the app sets no cookies and hands no personal data to advertisers.

What we never do

  • No selling your personal information for money
  • No plaintext passwords stored
  • No reading of your journal by anyone, and no journal, prayer-log or location data passed to advertisers
  • No ads inside the Quran reader, the Qibla compass or during a prayer alert

How long we keep things

Local data stays on your device until you clear it. A sync account and its backup are kept until you delete them. Advertising cookies last as long as the ad partners' own cookie lifetimes set out in their policies, and your consent choice is remembered until you change it.

Deleting your data

You can clear everything stored on a device with Sync → Clear this device. To have a cloud-sync account and its backup deleted, email linkg540@gmail.com from the address you registered with, and it will be removed.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Advertising is not intended to be personalised for anyone under the age of consent in their country. Cloud sync is optional and requires an email address.

Security

The app is served over HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as salted PBKDF2 hashes. Local data never leaves your device unless you create an account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change with it. Material changes will be noted in the app.
