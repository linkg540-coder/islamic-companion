Privacy Policy

Islamic Companion · last updated 16 November 2026

Islamic Companion is a free app with no ads and no advertising trackers. This page explains exactly what is stored, where it goes, and how to remove it.

What stays on your device

Settings, bookmarks, tasbih counts, journal entries, check-ins and cached Quran text are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. They are not uploaded anywhere unless you create a cloud-sync account.

Location

Your location is used to calculate prayer times and the qibla direction. If you grant location access, coordinates are sent to the Aladhan prayer-times API and to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service to name your town. If you decline, the app uses a rough IP-based estimate from ipinfo.io or ipwho.is. Coordinates are also included in prayer-alert settings only when you enable notifications.

Optional cloud sync

If you create an account, the app stores your email address, a salted PBKDF2 hash of your password (never the password itself), a session token, and a backup copy of your app data. The backup is used only to restore your data on your own devices. Login events (time, email, device type) are recorded for account security.

Notifications

If you enable prayer or journal alerts, your browser's push subscription, timezone, approximate coordinates and alert preferences are stored so the reminder can be delivered. You can turn alerts off at any time with the bell icon, or in your device settings.

Third-party services

  • Aladhan — prayer times and qibla calculation
  • AlQuran.cloud — Quran text, translations and recitation audio
  • cdn.islamic.network / everyayah.com — recitation audio
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim, Overpass) — place search and nearby mosques
  • jsDelivr — tafsir text
  • Base44 — account sync, backups and notification delivery
  • Groq (GPT-OSS-120B) — generates AI Sheikh answers; the text of your question is sent for processing and is not tied to your account

Each of these services receives only what is needed for its request. The app sets no advertising or analytics cookies.

What we never do

  • No ads, no ad networks, no selling data
  • No plaintext passwords stored
  • No reading of your journal by anyone

Deleting your data

You can clear everything stored on a device with Sync → Clear this device. To have a cloud-sync account and its backup deleted, email linkg540@gmail.com from the address you registered with, and it will be removed.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and collects no more information from children than from anyone else. Cloud sync is optional and requires an email address.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change with it.
