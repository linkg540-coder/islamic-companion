Surah An-Naas — Mankind

6 versesMeccanSurah 114 of 114

Arabic with the Saheeh International translation. Tap into the app for recitation, per-verse tafsir and 15 translations.

1
قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ ٱلنَّاسِ
Say, "I seek refuge in the Lord of mankind,
2
مَلِكِ ٱلنَّاسِ
The Sovereign of mankind.
3
إِلَٰهِ ٱلنَّاسِ
The God of mankind,
4
مِن شَرِّ ٱلْوَسْوَاسِ ٱلْخَنَّاسِ
From the evil of the retreating whisperer -
5
ٱلَّذِى يُوَسْوِسُ فِى صُدُورِ ٱلنَّاسِ
Who whispers [evil] into the breasts of mankind -
6
مِنَ ٱلْجِنَّةِ وَٱلنَّاسِ
From among the jinn and mankind."
← Al-Falaq
