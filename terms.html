What this app is

Islamic Companion is a tool for prayer times, Quran reading and listening, qibla direction, duas, hadith, journaling and general Islamic study. The core worship features are free and require no account. The app is supported by advertising on some screens and, in future, by optional paid features that will always be clearly labelled before you pay for anything.

Religious guidance

Prayer times are calculated estimates. The AI Sheikh provides educational summaries of positions across the schools of thought and cites sources, but it is not a mufti, its answers can contain mistakes, and nothing in the app is a fatwa. For rulings that affect your worship or daily life, consult a qualified scholar.

Content and services

Quran text, translations, recitation audio, tafsir and prayer-time data come from third-party services (AlQuran.cloud, Aladhan, cdn.islamic.network, everyayah.com, jsDelivr). If one of those services is unavailable, the related feature may be temporarily unavailable too. Cached copies let common features keep working offline.
